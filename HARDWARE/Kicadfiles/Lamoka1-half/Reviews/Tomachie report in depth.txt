7.2  Switch DFT Analysis
Switches for mode selection are useful for development and manual debug, but production test environments require electrical override capability. A latching switch (DIP, slide, toggle) holds the signal at the rail it closes onto until somebody moves it, so ATE cannot drive past the contact: it needs a test point and a series isolation resistor between the switch and that rail. A momentary switch (push button) does not hold the signal — the bias resistor sets the level again as soon as the button is released — so it needs only a test point for ATE stimulus access.

14.12  PCOLA/SOQ Testability
PCOLA/SOQ scores how well the configured test methods cover each component and each connection. PCOLA evaluates five device-level properties: Presence, Correctness, Orientation, Live (functional), and Alignment. SOQ evaluates three connection-level properties: Shorts detection, Opens detection, and solder joint Quality. Scores are on a 0–100,000 scale where 100,000 means every property is fully covered. The Combined score is the average of PCOLA and SOQ.
